Your pictures

Then, onto your radio

Copy the downloaded PNGs to the SD card’s IMAGES folder. Each filename must match the name shown by KSE — for example, TREX 700N.png. For OMP Auto, use OMP M1.png and OMP M2.png.

Restart the radio after replacing pictures. This clears its stored image data.

A few helpful details

These sizes fit the current full-screen KSE layouts, with a small two-pixel margin. Smaller widget areas may still scale the picture. Previews have a dark background so transparent edges are visible; that background is not added to your files.

“Both” creates separate KSE4 and KSE5 folders in the ZIP. If you use both dashboards with the same picture on one SD card, copy the KSE5 version; KSE4 can scale it down.

Only transparent margins are trimmed. Painted backgrounds stay, and enlarging a small picture cannot restore missing detail. Pictures are resized with a high-quality filter and saved as transparent PNGs. BMP files become PNGs.

Choose pictures again to replace the current batch. Duplicate filenames get a number at the end; rename those before copying to the radio. Files over 20 MB or 16 million pixels are skipped. Keep this HTML file on your computer and open it in a current version of Edge, Chrome, Firefox or Safari whenever you need it. Nothing to install.
